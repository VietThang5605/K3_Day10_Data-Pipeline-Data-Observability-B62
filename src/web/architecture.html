3-Phase Data Pipeline Flowchart

Phase 1

Baseline Ingestion & Eval

  • Fetch 24 scholarly papers via Crossref REST API.
  • Filter out non-Latin scripts (Russian paper dropped).
  • Build 23 clean English papers dataset.
  • Index into ChromaDB papers-baseline collection.
  • Evaluate on 10 frozen questions (Hit Rate: 100%, Accuracy: 90%).
Phase 2

Controlled Corruption

  • Inject 4 controlled corruption scenarios:
  • 1. Blank summary (Hi-RAG q1).
  • 2. Add culinary noise (CM-RAF q2, JADE q7).
  • 3. Stale date (5 papers backdated to 2000).
  • 4. Duplicates & missing paper_id.
  • Hit Rate drops to 90%, Accuracy drops to 70%.
Phase 3

Snapshot Repair & Matrix

  • Trigger automated repair from raw snapshot JSON.
  • Re-clean and re-index into papers-repaired.
  • Quality & Freshness checks recover to 100% PASSED.
  • Hit Rate & LLM Judge Accuracy recover to 100% / 90%.
  • Render 3-State Comparative Matrix Report.
